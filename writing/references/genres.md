# Genre notes

## Scientific papers and thesis
Use `structures.md` (ABT abstract, funnel introduction, LD results, LDR discussion with "So what?").

## Rebuttal letters (responding to reviewers)
- Structure per comment: reviewer comment (quoted) -> response -> what changed and where (section/page/line).
- Response pattern: acknowledge briefly -> answer directly (agree, clarify or respectfully disagree with reason) -> state the change.
- Thank reviewers once at the start, not in every response.
- Polite, direct, factual. No grovelling, no defensiveness.
- Lea states her position on each comment. Never decide on her behalf whether to concede or argue a point. If her position on a comment is missing, ask.

## Peer review reports (Lea as reviewer)
- Summary of the work and its main claim (2-4 sentences) -> overall assessment -> major points -> minor points.
- Each point: what the issue is, why it matters, what would fix it.
- Constructive and specific.

## Slides (PowerPoint)
- One message per slide; the title is a full-sentence claim (the message), not a topic label.
- Body: short phrases, parallel structure, few bullets. No paragraphs.
- Slides in sequence follow a story (ABT / OCAR); the last slide states the take-home message.
- Offer speaker notes only if asked.

## Casual writing (emails, messages, short posts)
- Purpose or request in the first sentence.
- Short, friendly, plain. Clear next step or question at the end.
- Same rules on naked pronouns and concise words apply.
